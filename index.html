Chaeyong Park

Assistant Professor

Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Korea University

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Korea University, and leading the AXIS Lab (Augmented eXperience & Interactive Systems Laboratory). I am also affiliated with the Department of Artificial Intelligence and the Department of Data Science at Korea University.

My research focuses on haptic rendering for realistic, immersive sensations through multimodal approaches — integrating vibrotactile, thermal, and impact feedback with visual and auditory cues in VR/AR systems.

Research Lab

AXIS Lab — Augmented eXperience & Interactive Systems

We create experiences that go beyond screens to engage every sense. Our research spans haptic technology, next-generation VR/AR, and novel interface prototyping — designing and building systems that bridge the physical and digital worlds.
